Objectives

  • Disassemble and identify functional components and their roles.
  • Determine materials and justify selection via engineering tests.
  • Map manufacturing sequences for individual components.
  • Produce consolidated technical documentation and process visualization.

  • Energy conversion: motor & armature.
  • Heat generation & control: heating coil and MICA board.
  • Air movement and direction: impeller, airflow director.
  • Output & user interface: nozzle, buttons, housing.

Manufacturing

Manufacturing Workflow

Process stages are consolidated for the assembly, from raw-material preparation through final testing and calibration.

Raw material prep: Rolling, Alloying, Extrusion, Lamination.
Primary forming: Injection Molding, Deep Drawing, Wire Drawing, Cold Heading.
Secondary ops: Trimming, Grinding, Heat Treatment, Surface Finishing.
Sub-assembly: Winding, Brush Assembly, Press-fit components.
Final assembly & testing: Mechanical assembly, electrical functional testing.
